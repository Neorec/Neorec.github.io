Privacy Policy

Sort Squishies by Neo PlayStudio · Effective October 29, 2026

The short version
  • We don't ask for your name or email, and there are no accounts.
  • Your game progress stays on your device. We never receive it.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads, as explained below.
  • An optional "Remove Ads" purchase is handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • Sort Squishies is meant for players aged 13 and older.

1. Who we are

Sort Squishies (the "App") is made by Neo PlayStudio ("we", "us"), an independent game studio based in Canada. If you have any questions about this policy or your information, contact us at powderhornet@hotmail.com. This address also reaches the person responsible for privacy at Neo PlayStudio.

2. Information stored only on your device

To save your game, the App keeps the following on your device: your level progress, stars, coins, daily puzzle streak, collected squishies, pet progress, shop unlocks, whether you bought Remove Ads, and settings (such as sound and music). This information is not sent to us and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes it permanently.

Android may include this game data in your device's own backups (for example, to your Google account) if you have backups turned on. That is controlled by your device settings and Google's terms, not by us.

3. Information we collect

Neo PlayStudio does not collect, store or sell personal information about you. The App has no sign-in, chat, or social features, and it does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads, including optional "rewarded" videos that you can choose to watch for in-game bonuses. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. When ads are requested or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the App may automatically collect and share with Google information such as:

InformationUsed for
Your device's advertising ID (a resettable ID provided by Android)Showing ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, measuring ad results, preventing fraud
IP address, which can suggest your approximate location (such as country or city)Showing ads relevant to your region, legal compliance, fraud prevention
Device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version)Showing ads that work on your device, measuring performance

Depending on your choices and where you live, ads may be personalized (based on your interests) or non-personalized (based on general information, such as the content of the App and your approximate location). We have limited ads in the App to content suitable for teens.

Google's use of this information is governed by Google's own policies. You can read more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: on most Android devices, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Turn off personalized ads: use the same Ads settings, or visit Google's My Ad Center.
  • Players in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form the first time they open the App, and can change their choice at any time under Settings → Privacy & ad choices in the App.

5. In-app purchases

The App offers an optional one-time purchase, "Remove Ads". Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play: we never see or store your payment card or billing details. When you buy, Google Play tells the App that the purchase was completed (including a purchase token and order number) so the App can turn off pop-up ads and restore your purchase if you reinstall. This confirmation is checked on your device and is not sent to us. Google's handling of your payment information is covered by the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

6. Children

Sort Squishies is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the App, please contact us at powderhornet@hotmail.com and we will help address it, including working with Google where ad data is involved.

7. Where information is processed

Google may process advertising information on servers in Canada, the United States and other countries, which may have different data protection laws from where you live. Google is responsible for safeguarding the information it processes.

8. How long information is kept

We don't keep any of your information. Game data on your device stays until you delete the App or its data. Google keeps advertising information according to its own retention policies.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), Quebec's privacy law, the EU or UK GDPR, or U.S. state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, to withdraw consent, or to object to certain uses, such as personalized advertising. Because Neo PlayStudio does not hold personal information about you, most requests are best handled through the ad choices above or directly with Google. You are always welcome to contact us at powderhornet@hotmail.com and we will help you. You may also contact the privacy regulator where you live, such as the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

10. Security

Your game data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we never collect your game data in the first place.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if we add new features. We'll update the "Effective" date at the top, and if a change is significant we will let you know in the App or in its Google Play listing.
